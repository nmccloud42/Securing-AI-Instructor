
# Securing AI — Course Roadmap

The course consists of seven modules completed on your **assigned cyber range machine** in **VS Code**. Use the **course shortcut on the desktop** to open the workspace. Before each module, make your own working copy of the student notebook.

| Module | Topic | What You Will Study | Module Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to AI Security | AI/ML fundamentals, attack surfaces, adversarial examples, poisoning, prompt injection, and AI security failures | [Open](Module_1_Introduction/README.md) |
| 2 | Threat Modeling AI Systems | Assets, trust boundaries, STRIDE, DREAD, FMEA, and risk prioritization | [Open](Module_2_Threat_Modeling/README.md) |
| 3 | Supply Chain and Data-Level Attacks | Training data integrity, poisoning, label flipping, clean-label attacks, drift, and mitigation | [Open](Module_3_Data_Level_Attacks/README.md) |
| 4 | Model Exploitation Techniques | Evasion, FGSM/PGD, extraction, inference, inversion, backdoors, and defenses | [Open](Module_4_Model_Exploitation/README.md) |
| 5 | LLM Security and Prompt Injection | Direct/indirect injection, jailbreaking, RAG, agent/tool misuse, and LLM defenses | [Open](Module_5_LLM_Security/README.md) |
| 6 | Secure Deployment and Infrastructure | AI pipelines, identities, secrets, containers, model integrity, and monitoring | [Open](Module_6_Secure_Deployment/README.md) |
| 7 | AI Security Operations and Defense | Logging, detection, response, recovery, and final defense capstone | [Open](Module_7_AI_Defense/README.md) |

## Repeat This Workflow for Every Module

1. Open the course using the **desktop shortcut**.
2. Read the module `README.md`.
3. **Copy** `ModuleX_Student.ipynb` into your designated personal work location.
4. Rename your copy `ModuleX_Student_FirstName_LastName.ipynb`.
5. Open the copy in VS Code, complete the tasks, and save regularly.
6. Submit your completed `.ipynb` file to the instructor.
